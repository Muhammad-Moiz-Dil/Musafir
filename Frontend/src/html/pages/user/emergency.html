🛡️ Travel Safety Guidelines

📱
Keep Emergency Contacts Saved
Save local police, nearest hospital, and PTDC helpline in your phone before departing. Share your itinerary with someone at home, including expected return dates.
💧
Carry Drinking Water
Mountain areas may have limited clean water access. Carry at least 2 litres per person per day. Purification tablets are recommended for trekking trips.
🏥
First Aid Kit is Essential
Remote mountain areas are far from hospitals. Carry a basic first aid kit with bandages, antiseptic, altitude sickness tablets, painkillers, and oral rehydration salts.
🌦️
Check Weather Before You Go
Mountain weather is unpredictable. Check forecasts on PMD (Pakistan Meteorological Department) before travelling to high-altitude areas. Never ignore storm warnings.
🚗
Vehicle Safety
Rent a 4x4 vehicle for mountain roads. Ensure tyres are in good condition. Never drive at night on mountain passes. Carry a spare tyre and basic tools.
📡
Communication in Remote Areas
Mobile signal is weak or absent in many mountain regions. Inform someone of your route and check-in times. Satellite communicators are recommended for serious treks.
🏔️
Altitude Sickness Prevention
Ascend gradually. Do not go above 500m elevation gain per day. Drink plenty of water. If you feel severe headache, nausea or confusion, descend immediately and seek medical help.
